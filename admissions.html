Questions

Good to know.

Which course is right for me?

If you are new to computers, DCA is a good start. For accounting work, choose Tally. For a degree or postgraduate course, consider BCA, B.A., B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, M.A., M.Sc, MSW, PGDCA or PG Yoga. Our team can advise you.

What are the fees and course durations?

These depend on the course. Please contact the college office or send an enquiry for the current details.

Can I visit before I decide?

Yes. You are welcome to visit the college in Khaddi, see the campus and speak with the staff.
