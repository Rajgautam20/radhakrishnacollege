About the college

Computer education, made accessible.

Radha Krishna Computer Education College in Khaddi helps students learn the computer skills that employers and further study ask for.

Director's message

Education that opens every door.

Dear students and parents,

Welcome to Radha Krishna Computer Education College, Khaddi. Our purpose is simple: to bring quality higher education and practical computer skills to every young person in our area, so that nobody has to leave home to build a good future.

From DCA and Tally to BCA, B.A., B.Sc, B.Com, BBA and on to M.A., M.Sc, MSW, PGDCA and PG Yoga, we offer a clear path at every stage. Our teachers guide each student with care, and we encourage you to learn by doing, to ask questions and to believe in your own potential.

I invite you to join our family. Work hard, stay curious, and we will stand with you at every step of your journey.
